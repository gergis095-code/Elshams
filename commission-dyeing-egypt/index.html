Commission dyeing

Commission dyeing in Egypt: your cloth, our dyehouse.

Garment makers and textile mills send their own greige cloth to El Shams in 10th of Ramadan. We dye it to the shade they approve, finish it to their spec and send back graded rolls. You keep the fabric sourcing. We handle the colour.

How it works

Dyeing services for garment manufacturers and mills.

Commission dyeing means you own the cloth and pay us for the processing. People in the trade also call it job work dyeing or toll dyeing. It suits buyers who already have a fabric source they trust, or who hold greige stock and want it dyed in batches as orders come in.

It's different from buying finished fabric. Here the cloth stays yours from start to finish. We receive it, check it, dye it, finish it and send it back. Our price covers the work, not the fabric.

Who sends us their cloth

  • Ready-made garment factories that buy greige and need it dyed to their buyer's shade
  • Knitting mills that don't run their own dyehouse
  • Traders who hold greige stock and dye it to order
  • Brands that want to keep control of where their fabric comes from

What we do to your cloth

Every lot goes through the same steps as our own fabric. First it's inspected and pre-treated (scoured and bleached) so it takes colour evenly. Then it's dyed on one of our 15 dyeing machines, chosen to fit the cloth and the lot size. After that it can go to rotary printing if you need a pattern, and to the finishing line for width, shrinkage and handle. The dyehouse runs at 40+ tons per day.

Getting a price

What to send for a quote.

The more we know about the greige, the faster and more accurate the price.

  • Fabric type and composition, for example 100% cotton single jersey or a cotton/viscose blend
  • Weight (GSM) and width of the greige as it will arrive
  • Quantity per colour, with a minimum of 250 kg per colour
  • Target shade: a pantone number, a lab dip or a physical swatch
  • Finishing needs: finished width, shrinkage target and handle
  • Delivery: where the finished rolls should go

With that, a price and a lead time usually come back within two working days.

Why your own cloth matters

A shade is only right on the fabric it was matched on.

A colour that looks perfect on a swatch card can look different on your fabric. Fibre, weight and pre-treatment all change how the dye takes. That's why we lab dip on your actual greige whenever we can. It's also why we don't take lots below 250 kg per colour.

If you'd rather buy finished fabric instead, we can do that too. See fabric dyeing in Egypt for our own dyed range, or knitted fabric made in our mill.

Common questions

Before you send your cloth

What is commission dyeing?

You own the greige cloth and send it to us. We dye and finish it to your shade and spec, then return it. You pay for the processing, not for the fabric.

What is the minimum quantity?

250 kg per colour. Below that a jet runs under its working load and the shade is harder to hold, so we say so before we take the order.

Which fibres can you dye?

Cotton, viscose, polyester and blends. If your cloth is something else, send the composition and we'll tell you honestly whether our line suits it.

How does shade approval work?

Send a pantone reference, a lab dip or a physical swatch. We match it on your cloth and send the lab dip back. Bulk dyeing starts only after you approve it.

Can you finish the cloth as well?

Yes. Our finishing line has 5 stenters, 2 closed compactors, a sanforizing machine, a singeing machine, 2 raising machines and a shaving machine, so the cloth can come back ready to cut.

Can you print on our cloth too?

Yes. We run 2 rotary printing machines with a capacity of 70,000 metres per day, so a dyed ground can be printed before it goes to finishing.

How long does a commission dyeing order take?

It depends on the quantity, the shade and the finishing you need. We confirm a firm lead time with the price, usually within two working days of getting your spec.
