What we knit

A knitting factory in Egypt that also dyes and finishes.

Most buyers looking for a knit fabric supplier in Egypt end up dealing with two or three companies. One knits the greige, another dyes it, a third finishes it. Every handover adds days, and every handover is a place where the shade or the shrinkage can drift. At El Shams the knitting floor sits next to the dyehouse and the finishing line, so your order moves through one building and one team.

The knitting floor runs at 20 tons a day. The greige it produces goes straight into the same six-stage line as every other order: intake and inspection, pre-treatment, dyeing, printing where the order needs it, finishing, then QC and dispatch.

Fabrics we knit

  • Single jersey: the everyday fabric for T-shirts and tops. It's light with a smooth face, and it's the base for a lot of printed work.
  • Rib: stretchy across the width. It's used for cuffs, collars, neck trims and close-fitting tops.
  • Interlock: a double knit with a smooth face on both sides. It's heavier and more stable than single jersey, so it suits polo shirts and children's wear.
  • Fleece: knitted first, then raised on our raising machines to give a soft, warm back. It's the fabric behind sweatshirts, hoodies and joggers.

Fibres and blends

We knit cotton, viscose, polyester and blends. Send the yarn count and composition you want. Or tell us the end use and the weight you're after, and we'll suggest a construction that fits.

From yarn to roll

How a knitted fabric order moves.

Six steps, all inside our mill in 10th of Ramadan, Sharkia.

01

Spec & quote

You send the fabric type, composition, weight, width and colours. We reply with a price and lead time, usually within two working days.

02

Knitting

Your greige is knitted on our own floor, so there's no waiting for a third-party knitter to deliver.

03

Inspection & pre-treatment

The greige is checked, then scoured and bleached so it takes colour evenly across the width.

04

Dyeing or printing

Dyed in jets or banana jets to your approved lab dip, or rotary printed with your design.

05

Finishing

Stenter for width and heat setting, compactor for shrinkage, and raising and shaving for fleece.

06

QC & dispatch

Every roll is inspected, graded and packed to destination spec, and logged against your order.

Why one mill

What you get when the knitter also dyes the cloth.

One supplier to answer to

If a roll shrinks or shades off, there's no argument between a knitter and a dyehouse about whose fault it was. It's one company, and every stage is logged against your order.

No time lost between companies

The greige doesn't sit in a warehouse or on a truck between suppliers. It goes from the knitting floor to the dyehouse in the same building.

Shrinkage set for knits

Knitted fabric moves in length after washing. Our closed compactors stabilise it, and residual shrinkage is checked against the figure in your spec.

Certified and auditable

ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018. Those are the three certificates European and Gulf buyers ask about at audit.

Minimum order & quotes

What to send us.

Dyed knitted fabric follows the same minimum as the rest of the dyehouse: 250 kg per colour. Below that a jet runs under its working load and the shade is harder to hold, so we'd rather tell you up front.

To get a quote, send us:

  • Fabric type (single jersey, rib, interlock or fleece) and composition
  • Weight in GSM and the finished width you need
  • Colours, with a pantone number, lab dip or physical swatch for each
  • Quantity per colour and where the rolls are going

A firm price and lead time usually come back within two working days. If you'd rather start with dyeing only, our commission dyeing service takes in greige you already own.

Common questions

Before you send a spec

What knitted fabrics do you make?

Single jersey, rib, interlock and fleece, in cotton, viscose, polyester and blends. If you need a construction that isn't on that list, send the details and we'll tell you honestly whether our floor can run it.

How much can you knit?

The knitting floor runs at 20 tons per day. The dyehouse in the same mill runs at 40+ tons per day, and rotary printing at 70,000 metres per day.

Do you dye and finish the knitted fabric too?

Yes. Knitted greige goes from our knitting floor to our own jets and banana jets, then to the stenters and compactors. You get finished rolls that are ready to cut and sew.

What is the minimum order?

250 kg per colour for dyed fabric. Below that a jet runs under its working load and shade control suffers, so we'd rather say so before you order.

Can you match our colour?

Yes. Send a pantone reference, a lab dip or a physical swatch. We match it and send it back for your approval before any bulk dyeing starts.

How fast do I get a quote?

Usually within two working days of receiving the fabric type, composition, weight, width, colours and quantity per colour.

Do you export knitted fabric?

Yes. We serve customers in Egypt, Europe and the Middle East, and every roll is graded and packed to destination spec.
